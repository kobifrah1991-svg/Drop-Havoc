Drop Havoc ("the game") is made by Scitedra Games (Kobi Yifrach), Israel. Contact: scitedra.labs@gmail.com. The business name, number and address are a proposal, pending confirmation.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Google account email and first nameOnly if you choose "Sign in with Google"To keep your account (level, in-game cash, items) and league points. Other players see only your nickname, never your email.
Random guest IDWhen you play without signing inKept on your device and on our server so your guest progress is saved.
Nickname, character and cosmeticsWhen you playShown to other players in the match.
Gameplay statistics (kills, level, in-game cash, items)While you play onlineTo run the game and keep your progress. In-game cash has no real-money value and cannot be bought.
IP addressWhen you connect onlineTo run the connection and to block abuse or cheating.
Chat messagesWhen you chatDelivered to players in the same room and filtered automatically. Not stored, except when a player reports a message (kept for moderation).
Voice (microphone)Only while voice chat is onSent live to the other players in your room. We never record or store voice. The app asks for microphone permission first; you can refuse and still play.

Practice against bots works offline and sends no game data to us. In rooms with friends and in voice chat, devices connect directly (peer-to-peer, WebRTC), so other players' devices can see your IP address; a connection service (PeerJS) and, when a direct link fails, a relay server (Open Relay by Metered) pass the connection along without storing it.

What we do not do

  • No ads, no advertising ID, no tracking or analytics SDKs.
  • No real-money purchases.
  • We never sell personal data.

Services we use

  • Game server: Render (render.com).
  • Sign-in: Google / Firebase Authentication (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Game files and updates: GitHub Pages.

Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS / secure WebSocket; WebRTC is encrypted by design).

Retention

Account data is kept while the account is active and deleted 12 months after the last login, or sooner on request. Ban and moderation records are kept up to 12 months. These retention periods are proposals, pending confirmation.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your account in the game, from Settings and from the pause screen (under Settings). You can also email scitedra.labs@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Drop Havoc account", from the Google account you signed in with, or, for a guest account, with your nickname and roughly when you last played. We will answer email requests within 30 days.

Upon deletion of a Google or guest account, your profile, progress, inventory, the Firestore player document and its backups are deleted. Reports you sent are kept for moderation, but your sender id is replaced by a one-way hash (HMAC). Reports about you are deleted, unless there is an active ban against you, in which case they are kept with the same hashed id. Bans are not removed. Your email, user id, guest token, name and IP address are deleted in plaintext form; only hashes remain so that an existing ban can still be applied.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes the guest ID and local settings from your device, but it does not by itself delete the server copy.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 16 and over (proposed minimum age, pending confirmation). It has realistic weapons, violence and chat with other players. If you think a child gave us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
